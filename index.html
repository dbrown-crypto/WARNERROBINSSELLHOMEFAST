How fast can you buy my Warner Robins house?

+

We can often close in as little as 7 days, or we can work around your schedule if you need more time. After we receive your property info, we present a written cash offer within 24 hours. There are no bank approvals, appraisals, or financing contingencies to create delays.

What condition does my home need to be in?

+

Any condition is fine. We have purchased homes with foundation issues, fire damage, full hoarder situations, code violations, unpermitted work, and everything in between. You do not need to clean, repair, stage, or touch a single thing before we make our offer.

Do I need to make repairs before selling?

+

No repairs needed. We buy properties exactly as-is. You walk away and we handle whatever the property needs after closing. This saves you the time, money, and coordination of managing contractors before a sale.

How much will you offer for my house?

+

Our offers are based on the property's condition, location, and current Middle Georgia market values. Cash offers are typically below full retail listing price. However, when you factor in zero commissions, zero repair costs, and zero closing costs, many sellers find the net result is competitive — and they receive it in days rather than months. We are transparent about how we arrive at our numbers and always encourage you to compare your options.

Do I have to pay any fees or commissions?

+

Zero fees and zero commissions. We cover all closing costs, title fees, and transfer taxes. The offer amount we present to you is exactly what you receive at the closing table. No deductions and no surprises.

What areas do you buy houses in?

+

We buy houses throughout Middle Georgia and the surrounding counties, including Houston, Bibb, Peach, Twiggs, Jones, Monroe, Bleckley, Dodge, Laurens, Baldwin, and Pulaski counties. We serve Warner Robins, Macon, Perry, Centerville, Bonaire, Byron, Kathleen, Fort Valley, Milledgeville, Gray, Forsyth, Hawkinsville, Dublin, Cochran, Eastman, and all surrounding communities. If you are unsure whether we serve your area, reach out and we will let you know right away.

What if I am behind on payments or facing foreclosure?

+

We work with homeowners in foreclosure situations and can often close before a scheduled foreclosure sale date. Time is critical — the closer to your sale date, the fewer options you have. Contact us as soon as possible if foreclosure is a concern. Even if your timeline is very short, reach out and we will let you know what may be possible.

What happens after I contact you?

+

A simple four-step process:

  • You contact us (5 minutes) — Phone, text, or form. We ask a few basic questions about your property.
  • Property walkthrough (30 minutes) — We schedule a quick visit at your convenience. No need to clean or prepare anything.
  • Receive your cash offer (24 hours) — We present a fair written offer. No obligation, take your time to review.
  • Close on your timeline (7 or more days) — If you accept, we close when it works for you. You can walk away at any point before signing with no hard feelings.

Is there any obligation after requesting an offer?

+

None at all. Requesting a cash offer is completely free and carries zero obligation. You are under no pressure to accept, and there are no fees for requesting an offer. We simply want to give you the information you need to make the best decision for your situation.

Can you buy inherited or tenant-occupied properties?

+

Yes. We frequently work with inherited properties, estate sales, and homes with tenants still in place. These situations have additional complexity, and our team has experience navigating them. Reach out and we can walk through what the process looks like for your specific circumstances.
